# DholDeck — Punjabi DJ, Remix & Auto-Tune Studio

Live at **https://gurjeetbit.github.io/punjabi-dj/**

A browser DJ studio for Punjabi music. Everything runs on your device; songs are never uploaded.

- **3 decks**: BPM and beat-grid detection, sync, key lock, key shift, loops, brake, 3-band EQ, filter, echo and reverb.
- **Mashup Studio** (separate tab): unlimited songs. Paste YouTube / Apple Music links or song names as the running order, drop in the audio files (auto-matched by name), and build a mashup in one click: Bhangra Empire stage mix, DJ KSR-style wedding mega remix, DJ quick-cut mashup, club remix or non-stop dhol medley.
- **Note on links**: browsers can't extract audio from YouTube or Apple Music, so each linked song needs its audio file.
- **Sounds**: dhol, dholki, chimta, tumbi, algoza, sarangi, harmonium, khartal, ghungroo, tabla, sitar, santoor, shehnai, bansuri, tanpura, manjira, shankh, 808s and EDM effects, all synthesised live.
- **Beat maker**: 16-step dhol sequencer with melody layers in your chosen key.
- **Vocal booth**: live mic auto-tune and recordable shout pads.
- **Downloads**: FLAC lossless (48 or 96 kHz, 24-bit), WAV (up to 96 kHz, 24-bit) or MP3 320 kbps.

Files: `index.html` (the app) and `lame.min.js` (MP3 encoder, [lamejs](https://github.com/zhuker/lamejs), LGPL).
