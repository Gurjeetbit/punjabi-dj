Auto Remix — Stage Mix Builder

Load 2–3 songs into the decks, then build a staged medley the way competition bhangra mixes are cut: dhol intro, build-up, drops with dhol on top, a dhol & tumbi break, and a speed-up finale. Preview it live, then render a studio-quality WAV.

Beat Maker — Dhol & Melody Loops

16-step dhol sequencer plus Punjabi melody layers that follow the Key and Scale in the top bar. Tap a step: loud → soft → off. Hit SYNC on a deck to lock a song to this beat.

Sound Pads

Every sound is synthesised live at full quality and tuned to your key. Keyboard: Q–I and A–K play the first 16 pads of the open bank.

Vocal Booth — Live Auto-Tune

Sing or shout into your mic and the pitch snaps to the Key and Scale above. Retune speed 0 gives the hard, robotic Punjabi-pop sound; higher values sound natural. Use headphones.

Master FX

Shared echo and reverb (each deck, the mic and the pads send into them), plus a glue compressor and brick-wall limiter on the master so mixes stay loud and clean.

DholDeck runs entirely in your browser — your songs never leave your device. All instrument sounds are synthesised in real time; no samples are copied from any record. Downloads: FLAC or WAV lossless up to 96 kHz / 24-bit, or MP3 at 320 kbps.
